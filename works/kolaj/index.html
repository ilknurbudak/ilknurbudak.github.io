Motion — camera

Each piece prints its composition larger than the frame: a piece map. Every piece has its own camera, and each layer follows it at its own depth — back slow, front fast. The seed drives only the motion; it never touches the composition.

PathThe camera keeps moving along a closed path across the map. The state of being on the way.
FallVertically dominant. The camera accelerates downward, hits bottom, rebounds, falls again.
JumpHard cuts dominate. The camera leaps between regions, drifting slightly between leaps.
SplitThe frame splits in two or four; each part shows a different region of the map.
PrintLayers arrive one by one in each piece. Each new layer flows into place.
